# The Forever Can visual system

Adapted from the editorial ecommerce language of `smxlventures-create/toala-towels-site`, then rewritten for wallpaper, home décor, and a pink wastebasket mark. Do not copy the TOALA wordmark or towel claims.

## Direction

Bold editorial ecommerce: oversized type, hard black rules, deliberate color blocking, and wastebasket photography treated as campaign imagery rather than a polite catalog grid.

## Typography

- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Space Grotesk
- Utility labels: DM Mono

Headlines stay huge, slightly skewed in the hero, and break aggressively. Labels stay mono and uppercase.

## Palette

Toala starting point, then a house pink from the Instagram mark:

- Ink `#0d0d12`
- Paper `#eee9df`
- Sun `#ffd84d`
- Cobalt `#2b5bac`
- Lilac `#c7b5ff`
- Tomato `#df5f3d`
- Pink `#f4b6c8`
- Blush `#f7d6e0`
- Rose `#c45a7a`

Product pages tint the shell with each listing’s light/dark pair. The shared chrome stays Paper / Ink / Pink.

## Brand mark

`/public/forever-mark.svg` is the house badge: a candy-pink circle, a white tapered wastebasket, and WALLPAPER / WASTEBASKET on the ring. It is not the TOALA wordmark and must not be typeset as a towel logo.

The header lockup is the badge plus stacked Archivo Black “THE FOREVER CAN”. The footer uses the same badge and a giant FOREVER CAN line in Sun on Ink.

`/public/favicon.svg` is a crop of the pink circle and can.

## Product photography

Campaign stills live under `/public/images/`. They show a tapered wastebasket wearing wallpaper in powder rooms, studios, and collection lineups. No TOALA lettering. No towel geometry.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop uses split-screen editorial compositions and two-column shop grids. At 900 px and below, pages stack, navigation becomes a labeled menu, and pricing controls go full width. At 520 px the header shop/cart pill hides and fact strips become a single column.
