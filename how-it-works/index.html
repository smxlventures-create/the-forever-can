The Forever Can is a clear acrylic cylinder, an interchangeable metal insert (nickel or gold), and a cutting template. The paper is whatever leftover you kept from the wall.

01

CLEAR ACRYLIC

A cylindrical shell you can see through. The wrap sits behind it — not glued on.

02

METAL INSERT

Brushed nickel or gold. Nests inside and holds the paper. Pick the finish on Etsy.

03

CUTTING TEMPLATE

Place it on leftover wallpaper, art, or a photo. Trace. Cut. Overlap the ends.
