THE
PATENT.

Published 3 October 2024 as Decorative Trash Receptacle System and Kit, from provisional 63/456,054. Inventor and applicant: Patricia H. Gilley. The invention is change: a clear shell and a metal insert that display wallpaper without manufacturing a glued souvenir.

01

CLEAR SHELL

A transparent outer cylinder becomes a viewing window for leftover wallpaper, art, photos, or holiday paper.

02

THE TEMPLATE

Trace leftover paper from the wall you just hung. Cut. Overlap. Drop it in. That remnant is the wrap.

03

NICKEL OR GOLD

A brushed-nickel stainless or gold-finish insert nests inside and holds the décor. Chosen on the Etsy listing — not sold here as fake SKUs.

04

FOREVER

No adhesive. Swap the paper when you redecorate. The can lasts. That is the name.
